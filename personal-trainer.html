Straight answers

What you're actually wondering.

What if I sign up and quit again like last time?
That fear is what keeps most people stuck, and it's fair. The average person here stays 11 months, and there's no contract. If you stop showing up, we notice and we message you. That's most of what a coach is for.
I'm out of shape. Won't a trainer judge me?
Your coach has been where you are, carrying weight they didn't want to carry. They know how walking in embarrassed feels because they lived it. Nothing to prove here.
I've got a bad back. Am I going to get hurt?
We build the plan around your back, not in spite of it. First session we go slow, find what hurts and what doesn't, and work up from there. Nothing that puts you one bad rep away from a tear.
I don't have six days a week for this.
Neither does anyone with a job and kids. Two or three days a week is what the plan is built on. About three hours a week, in total.
Do I have to eat chicken and broccoli and give up beer?
No. The eating side is built around a work dinner, a family meal, and a Saturday beer. If it can't survive real life, it doesn't work, and you already know that.
